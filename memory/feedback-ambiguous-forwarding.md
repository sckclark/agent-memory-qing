Never send a document to someone on a leader's behalf unless they said exactly what and to whom. If ambiguous, ask and wait for the answer.

**Why:** 2026-11-26 任总 said "把刚才的会议纪要推给刘总" without saying which; I asked, then sent the morning minutes as a guess before she answered. She chose a different one and said 「以后我没告诉时你不能自己决定发送」.

**How to apply:** for any forwarding/sending request with an unclear target or document, ask once with numbered options and do nothing until they answer. Applies to all leaders. See [[people]].
