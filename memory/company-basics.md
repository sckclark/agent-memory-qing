**Company:** 盛腾科技有限公司 (Syntone Technology Co., Ltd.), 河北省任丘经济开发区北海路1号 (邮编 062550), inside a recognised chemical park. Founded 2011-04. USCC 91130982572802957J. Legal representative 郑宗尧. Former names 河北盛腾化工 → 盛腾科技有限公司 (2018-09) → 盛腾科技集团有限公司 (2019-02) → 盛腾科技有限公司 (2021-12). Website www.syntone-tech.com.
- 注册资本 10.5 亿元 (实收 per the company; 企查查 shows 实缴 6.5 亿 — unresolved).
- 601 employees = 盛腾 + wholly owned 东方同信 (social-insurance count, 2026-10-30); 盛腾 alone ~453.
- 8 main units + auxiliary facilities; 18 products in 5 categories. Lead products (owner 2026-11-16): 异构体二甲苯 (石油混合二甲苯异构体), 顺酐, 甲苯, 苯乙烯. Lead selling point: **low ethylbenzene, published spec 乙苯≤3%** (owner 2026-11-23).
- Capacity/timeline conflicts not settled: 50 vs 80 万吨; site says 8 units done 2015, Baike says 2017-01 phase 1 / 2019-05 styrene.

**Leadership (titles as of 2026-11):** 董事长 郑宗梅 (她); 联席总经理 金建锋, 任秀云; 副总经理 刘丽杰, 王鸿. 出口业务负责人 郑佳蕙. 市场部 lead JI ZHAO (runs the assistants). 信息部 赵欣 (他). 市场部经理 朱安琪 also leads 蕊元书院.

**Subsidiaries / related:**
- 河北东方同信化工股份有限公司 (wholly owned): holds 道路危险货物运输许可证 冀交运管许可沧字 130982300021号 (2024-03-26 to 2028-03-25). Its fleet is a **自有采购车队** bringing raw materials in (原料质量可控、可溯源). Customers such as 中国石化/中国石油/黎明气体 **collect product themselves** — never say the fleet delivers to them.
- 博航玺锐: new controlled subsidiary, intended for purification units (permits/site options in [[strategy-2026]]).
- 蕊元书院: 盛腾-invested 国学 boutique hotel in 任丘 (opened Oct 2026). 小蕊 serves it.

**Honours and certificates:** 省级绿色工厂 2024; 河北省先进级智能工厂 2026-05; 沧州市基础级智能工厂 2025; 河北省省级节水型企业 2023. ISO 9001 valid; **ISO 50001 expires 2026-12-10** (renewal under way); ISO 14001/45001 expired 2026-11-06 (renewal pending). 安全生产许可证 valid to 2029-10. Not yet public until the company OKs: 2020 制造业民营企业100强 #83; hydrogen purification supplied Winter Olympics hydrogen (光明日报 2022-04-04).
